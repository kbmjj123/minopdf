### Added

### Available Workflows

- PDF, image, text, HTML, Markdown, and ZIP-based conversion workflows.
- PDF organization tools, including merging, page extraction, rotation, and page-size normalization.
- Metadata viewing and cleanup workflows.
- Text, Markdown, page-image, and image-count extraction workflows.
